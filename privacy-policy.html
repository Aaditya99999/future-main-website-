Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 • Future Edge Digital Marketing

Future Edge Digital Marketing ("Future Edge", "we", "our", "us"), Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India, respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you work with us, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

Information you send us: when you use a form on this site (demo class booking, partnership application, newsletter sign-up), call us, or message us, we receive the details you share, such as your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, business details, and course or service preferences.

Client and student information: if you become a client or student, we may also hold billing details, GST number (if applicable), project information, and course records such as attendance, assignments, internship progress and certification status.

Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit) to keep the site secure and working.

2. How Our Website Forms Work

Our forms do not store your details on our website. When you submit the demo class or partnership form, your WhatsApp app opens with your details filled in, and they reach us only when you press send. The newsletter form opens your email app in the same way. Messages sent through WhatsApp or email are also handled under WhatsApp's (Meta's) and your email provider's own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to reply to enquiries, schedule consultations and demo classes, prepare proposals, deliver our marketing services and training programs, issue invoices, send newsletters you asked for, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Services

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. Staff who log in to the blog admin area receive a single session cookie that keeps them signed in. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy first.

5. Sharing Your Information

We share personal information only when needed to provide our services, for example with our hosting, email and payment providers, with partner firms for internships or placements you have agreed to, or when required by law. Everyone we share with must protect it.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client or student records for as long as our relationship continues plus any period required by tax and accounting laws. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or erase your personal information, withdraw consent you have given (for example, to stop receiving our newsletter), or nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. You also have the right to raise a grievance with us, and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

If a student is under 18, we collect their information only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No website or online service is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information (such as passwords or full bank details) through WhatsApp or email.

10. Contact and Grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email futureedgedigitalmarketing@gmail.com or call +91-6393213527. We will acknowledge your request and respond within the time required by law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website or services change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
